How it began

Built by a food scientist

Our founder, Kamran Ahmed Siddiqui, spent more than 20 years inside food factories for multinational brands, checking how things were really made.

Good Noura is built on what he learned there: careful sourcing, a recipe he'd happily feed his own family, and a label that tells you everything. Good peanut butter should taste like peanuts — not sugar syrup.
